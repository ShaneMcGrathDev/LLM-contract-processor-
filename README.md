## 🏗️ Technical challenges in the systems and processes landscape

Developing this project touches on an important nerve for me that I believe is so important in the technical landscape today. 

On the surface, file management may not seem like a compelling topic in business discourse but I strongly disagree! File management is an entrypoint into the discussion of the broader systems and processes landscape. This is fertile ground for technical professionals, especially software/data engineers to generate transformative value. Why do I say that? 

If you were to go into your business and mine for use cases/requirements, you would likely find many symptoms signalling a deeper problem and opportunity. Often times these problem spaces live in silos due to organizational and team structures, but I would argue these problem spaces are more interconnected than any of the individual silos realize. A holistic and strategic view of these problem spaces is often desperately needed.  

## 💥 Technical pitfalls today

Within this systems and processes landscape, despite unprecedented evolution of technological capabilities, much business process excellence and collaboration is still trapped inside inefficient workflows, let alone the path to futuristic capabilities not yet seen.

 Manual workflows are more than a mere inconvenience. They wear down stakeholders, drive up costs, and obstruct intuitive business execution. They can stifle the most talented and capable personnel from executing to their fullest potentials. This application shines a light on just one area of this broader theme. 

## 🏭 Giant tech and everything in between 
 The big box application systems commonly found in large organizations often leave behind many cracks and crevices of missed opportunity, leaving stakeholders to fill in the system and process design gaps wherever and however they can. Although, these "tiny" gaps could look like huge canyons to small/mid-size companies looking in from the outside, everything is relative! 
 
 These missed design/process considerations in complex environments seem unavoidable. But it also must be understood that these gaps compound into bigger effects with real business implications. Stakeholders often become process designers reluctantly because they have to. 
 
Grassroots design, development, and customization from the ground up is a golden opportunity, in my humble opinion. Full stack applications such as these inclusive of AI/ML executed well help drive towards the big goal. This is something I am passionate about as a business/software development topic. 

Applications such as these can help the broader population of business stakeholders, existing in varying degrees outside of the technical landscape, start to alleviate the system and process burden. So build things, show people things, talk to people about their experiences and learn from them, and help drive the transformation. Everyone is welcome to the transformation team and can play a role.
